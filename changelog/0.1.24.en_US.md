# Combat 0.1.24: toggle sprint on from the start

Toggle Sprint now starts on and remembers your choice, so you no longer press G every time you open the game.

- **On by default**: you sprint from the first step, without holding the sprint key.
- **Your choice is kept**: turning it off with G or in the Panel keeps it off the next time you play.
- **On servers with an anticheat**: with Omni Sprint on, you also sprint backwards and sideways from the start. Turn one of them off if a server punishes it.
