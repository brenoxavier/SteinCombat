# The list of allies and enemies can come from a URL

Your clan publishes one file on the internet and everyone points at it: allies, enemies, allied clans and enemy clans arrive ready, and a change of sides counts for everybody on the next time they join the server. This version also drops two things from the mod, at the request of the CraftLândia staff: hiding allies and the protection against hitting an ally — of both, only your own clan is left.

- **List from the internet**: in the Panel, under "List from the internet", turn the option on and write the address of a JSON file. It is downloaded when you join a server and on the "Refresh now" button, which shows how many names came with it.
- **It is the URL's list or yours, never both**: with the mode on, the handwritten lists are on hold — they stay saved and count again as soon as you turn the mode off. The mark-ally and mark-enemy keys say so in the chat instead of changing a list that is not in effect.
- **One address only, and the file says which list belongs to which server**: the lists on the outside count on every server, and the ones inside `servers` are added to them while you are on that server. It can be the server's address or its domain, which covers the subdomains. That way a single URL serves every server your clan plays on.
- **Without internet, the last one counts**: what was downloaded is kept and stays in effect while the site is down — and the chat says, as you join, that the list in effect is not a fresh one. Nothing waits on the answer: the game joins just the same.
- **Enemy clans**: the mark-enemy key with Shift marks the clan of whoever you are aiming at, and the Panel has "Add clan" under enemies too. Everyone with that tag becomes an enemy, even on a server where you have no clan. A nick marked by hand counts above their clan: whoever is on your ally list stays an ally.
- **Hiding and not hitting left the ally and stayed with the clan**: "Hide allies from the screen" became "Hide my clan from the screen", and "Don't hit allies" became "Don't hit my clan". Marked allies and allied clans keep their colour in the name, in the Tab list and above their head — they just no longer vanish from the screen or hold your hit back. Both depend on "My clan is allied" being on; with it off, they hide and protect no one.
- **Sprint in all directions is back** (option, off): with the sprint key held, backwards and sideways too. On a server it is detectable, and the mod says so in the chat when you turn it on.
- **Only Insert opens the Panel**: the mod's keys (mark ally, mark enemy) with no one under the crosshair no longer open any screen — a menu opening in the middle of a fight was the easiest way to stand still looking at it.

The list file is plain JSON, and everything in it is optional:

    { "allies":     ["Nick1", "Nick2"],
      "allyClans":  ["ABC", "[DEF]"],
      "enemies":    ["Nick3"],
      "enemyClans": ["XYZ"],
      "servers": {
        "play.example.com": { "allies": ["Nick4"], "enemyClans": ["QWE"] }
      } }

The Portuguese names work too (`aliados`, `clasAliados`, `inimigos`, `clasInimigos`, `servidores`), a clan tag can come with or without brackets, and a bad line is ignored without taking the rest of the list down. When the same name is on both sides, the server's wins over the outer one — the more specific is the one that counts.
