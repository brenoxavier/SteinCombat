# Hiding allies now respects "My clan is allied"

With that option off, your clan is not an ally for anything — not protected from your hits, not painted in the ally color — and yet it still vanished when you pressed the hide key. Not any more.

- **Exactly who the hit protection protects now vanishes**: the nicks you marked, the allied clans and, only with "My clan is allied" on, your own clan.
- **With the option off**, your clan stays on screen along with everyone else; allied clans and marked nicks still vanish.
