# One key takes your allies off the screen

Combat got a key that removes your clan and your allies from the screen, so you can see who is hitting you in the middle of a pile-up; press it again and everyone comes back.

- **Hide allies (H key)**: your clan, the allies you marked and the allied clans stop being drawn — body, armor, nametag and shadow. It is not the game's invisibility, which would still show the armor: for whoever is looking, there is nobody there. They stay in the Tab list and on the minimap, and hitting them is still cancelled by the ally protection. The key can be changed in Controls, and the same option is in the Panel.
- **Chat notice**: turning it on and off writes one line in the chat, in the same format as the other Combat messages, instead of flashing above the hotbar where the server writes too.
- **"Sprint in all directions" is gone**: it was the one feature in the mod a server anticheat could punish, and the little it gave does not pay for that risk. The sprint toggle key (G) is unchanged.
- **Needs Stein Loader 0.1.9**: the hide key uses a new engine hook, so the mod only loads with that version or newer. With an older one it shows up in the mod list with the reason written out, instead of silently going missing.
