# Combat 0.1.22: armor that stays on in big fights

Auto Armor and Auto Refill now keep up when many players hit you at once, even with a full inventory and a full chest.

- **No more bare pieces**: a worn piece is swapped without ever coming off first, so you are never unprotected during the swap. Only a piece that already broke is put on with a shift-click.
- **Fast fights**: with armor running out quickly, Auto Armor no longer slows down between swaps, and each piece is handled on its own. If the server refuses a swap, it tries again right away, in a different way.
- **Auto Refill with a full inventory and a full /bau**: it no longer gets stuck, and it no longer opens the chest in the middle of an armor swap.
- **Server clan plugin removed**: it was never part of the mod. Combat keeps reading clans from the server's own clan plugin (SimpleClans and the like), as before.
