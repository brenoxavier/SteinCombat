# Combat 0.1.18: reorganized Panel, Autoclick and a new feature list

Requires Stein Loader 0.1.28.

- **Reorganized Panel**: Combat, Movement, Team, Auto Armor, Auto Refill, Smart Effect and Auto Protect sections, with clearer names in all three languages (for example "Hit through allies", "Return worn pieces", "Swap", "After taking damage"). Existing settings keep working.
- **Autoclick** (new): clicks at the slider rate (5 to 100 CPS), exact, with no variation, like an external autoclicker: each click goes into the mouse queue like a real one, so the hit, the packets, the game's own hit delay and the HUD CPS counter all work the same. Two modes: while holding the left button, or always on. Only with no screen open, and last in line: it waits for armor, refill, food and potions, and the blocking sword.
- **New active feature list**: each line has a background and an accent bar, colors by category (combat, movement, team, automation), rainbow or a single color, and lines slide in and out. Each feature has "Show in list" to leave it out.
- **Show in list**: a checkbox on each feature's row, next to the key +; the key can be a mouse button.
- **Cleaner notices**: the action bar only shows on and off; the missing potion, armor, apple and chest notices are gone.
- **New defaults**: a fresh install starts with a PvP-ready setup.
- **Auto Protect is now its own feature**, with two parts, each with its own switch:
  - **Block in menus**: the sword blocking while a screen is open, as before.
  - **Low health block** (new): at low health in a fight, it grabs the sword and keeps blocking until health is back. While blocking, mcMMO's counterattack does not trigger and you take half damage. Works with Auto Armor, Regeneration and Smart Effect: armor first, then the apple, then blocking; Speed and Strength wait for health to come back.
- **Hit through allies**: hitting someone in your clan hits whoever is right behind them, in line with your aim and within reach.
- **Nearest ally**: new HUD element, next to nearest enemy.
