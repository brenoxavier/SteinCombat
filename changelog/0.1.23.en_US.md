# Combat 0.1.23: no missing piece with a full bag

Auto Refill now brings a missing armor piece even when your bag is full and there is nothing worn to swap.

- **Room for the missing piece**: with a full bag and no spare of a piece at all, another bag item goes to the chest in its place, so you no longer fight without that piece. Nothing is lost: the item stays in the chest.
- **The least useful item goes first**: blocks and other ordinary items first, then an extra spare of another piece, and only then something you fight with, like potions or food.
- **Your hotbar is never touched**: only items in the bag are moved.
