# Combat 0.1.21: on the public API

Combat now turns its sprint and hit switches on through the Stein Loader's public API, like any other mod, instead of a part of the Loader reserved for it.

- **Needs Stein Loader 0.1.25**: the Stein Client and the installer update the Loader on their own. Nothing changes in how Combat works, and your settings stay where they are.
