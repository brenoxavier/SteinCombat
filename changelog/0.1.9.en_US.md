# Keep sprint when hitting (optional, off)

1.8 drops your sprint on every hit that knocks back — the "sprint reset". You can now turn that off, with an option that starts off.

- **What it changes**: with it on, hitting no longer drops your sprint. Without it, the game behaves as always: sprint drops on the hit and, with the sprint toggle key (G), comes back on the next tick.
- **What it does not do**: sprint backwards or sideways. It only prevents the drop caused by the hit; stop moving forward, sneak, touch a wall or run out of food and the game drops the sprint as usual.
- **On a server, think twice**: the client tells the server when it starts and stops sprinting, and by never stopping, the server sees hit after hit with you sprinting — which is what anticheats look for. Turning it on shows that warning in the chat.
- **Needs Stein Loader 0.1.12**: the new option uses an engine feature that only exists from that version on. Update the Loader (with the installer) before the mod; with an older Loader, the mod shows up in the list with the reason written out, instead of breaking.
