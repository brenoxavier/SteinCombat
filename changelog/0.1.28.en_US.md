# Combat 0.1.28: Auto Walk and hidden allies

Walk without holding a key, and hide any ally, not only your clan, in the way that suits the fight, with a quiet sign on the ground of where they are.

- **Auto Walk**: press J and your character keeps walking forward; with Toggle Sprint on, it sprints. Press forward or back to stop.
- **Hide allies**: the hide key now covers every ally, not only your clan, in three styles: disappear, ghost (see-through, with the option to hide their name) and gone 100%, as if they were not there. In the ghost and gone styles your hits go through them to whoever is behind.
- **Presence marker**: with allies hidden, footprints that fade where they walk, or a square under their feet, in the ally's color or in black.
- **Ally Protect**: protection against hitting allies now covers every ally, not only your clan.
- **Removed**: Omni Sprint and Keep Sprint. Toggle Sprint stays.
