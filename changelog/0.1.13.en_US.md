# Allies and enemies other mods can see too

Nothing changes in Combat itself: the same lists, clans and colours. The difference is that it now tells Stein Loader who is an ally, an enemy or in your clan — and any mod can ask the Loader, not only the HUD.

- **Your colours count everywhere**: the clan, ally, enemy and neutral colours chosen here now also paint the HUD's Tab list headers.
- **Under the hood**: the mod no longer talks to Minecraft directly — keys, chat, screens, players and inventory go through Stein Loader.
- **Needs Stein Loader 0.1.23**: update with the installer before the mod.
