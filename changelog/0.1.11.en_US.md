# Allies turn into ghosts instead of vanishing

With the hide key (H) on, an ally who is not in your clan is now drawn as the game's own translucent silhouette — the one you see of players who are invisible and on your team. You can see who is hitting behind them, and they are still there: name above their head, and taking hits like anyone else.

- **It is the middle ground**: hiding allies left the mod in the previous version, at the request of the CraftLândia staff. The ghost gives back what was useful about it — seeing through a crowd — without taking anyone off the screen.
- **Your own clan still vanishes completely** with H, as it does today. Whoever vanishes does not become a ghost.
- **No armour, held item, fire or particles**: the silhouette comes out clean. A burning ally shows no flames, and their potion particles are gone too — otherwise the screen would keep exactly what the ghost exists to clear.
- **No red damage flash**: a ghost does not blink when it takes a hit — the screen really is clear.
- **Option "Allies turn into ghosts"**, in the Panel next to the hide toggle — it starts on. Turn it off and allies stay solid.
- **Needs Stein Loader 0.1.19**: the ghost drawing lives in the engine. Update with the installer before the mod.
